FlipTools privacy policy

Last updated 7 November 2026 · applies to FlipTools 2.1 and later

FlipTools works out whether an eBay item is worth reselling locally. It does that entirely inside your browser. It has no server, no account and no analytics, and it never sends what you do anywhere.

What it reads, and when

FlipTools reads a web page only when you click its button in the Chrome toolbar, and only the tab you clicked it on. Chrome grants that access one tab at a time; FlipTools has no standing access to any website.

  • On an eBay listing: the item title, the price and the shipping cost shown on the page.
  • On Facebook Marketplace or OfferUp search results: when you press “Read prices on this page”, the asking prices currently shown in the results.

It does not read anything else on those pages: not your messages, your profile, your account details, other people’s names or anything you type there. It never opens pages in the background, never signs in for you and never changes the page.

What it keeps

WhatWhereHow long
The item you’re working on: its title, search words and the three prices in the calculatorChrome’s session storage on your computerUntil you close Chrome or press “Start over”
Deals you choose to save: title, prices, profit, return and the dateChrome sync storage in your own Chrome profileUntil you delete them
Your two thresholds (minimum profit and return)Chrome sync storage in your own Chrome profileUntil you change them or remove the extension

If you have Chrome sync turned on, Google stores your saved deals and thresholds with the rest of your Chrome data so they appear on your other computers. The developer of FlipTools can’t see any of it.

What it shares

Nothing. FlipTools makes no network requests of its own. When you press “Marketplace” or “OfferUp”, it opens a normal search page on that site in a new tab, exactly as if you had typed the search yourself; from then on that site’s own privacy policy applies.

FlipTools does not sell data, does not use it for advertising, and does not use it for anything other than showing you the numbers on screen.

Removing your data

Delete saved deals with the × next to each one. Removing FlipTools from Chrome deletes everything it stored on your computer; synced copies can be cleared in Chrome under Settings → You and Google → Sync → Manage what you sync.
