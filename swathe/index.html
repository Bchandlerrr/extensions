Swathe privacy policy

Last updated 7 November 2026 · Swathe 1.0 and later

Swathe keeps nothing and sends nothing. It looks at the links inside the box you draw, does what you ask with them, and forgets them.

When it runs

Only on the page where you click the Swathe button or press its shortcut, and only until you open or copy your links, press Esc, or leave the page. Chrome gives it that page and no other; it has no standing access to any website.

What it looks at

While you drag, Swathe checks which links on the page fall inside your box: their web addresses and the words or image descriptions they show. It doesn’t read anything else on the page, such as text you’ve typed or your account details.

What happens to it

Open in tabs asks Chrome to open those addresses in new tabs. Copy links puts the addresses on your clipboard. That’s all. Swathe stores nothing (not even settings), has no server, no account and no analytics, and makes no network requests of its own. The pages you open are then governed by those sites’ own policies.

Swathe doesn’t sell, share or use any data for advertising or anything else.
